    <description>
PROBLEM: Harness discovery / quota probing spawns the gemini CLI on essentially every ddx work / ddx run startup (discovery probes all installed agent CLIs), and the spawned gemini process is NOT reaped — it reparents to init (ppid=1) and lingers for many hours. Observed on the dogfooding box: 123 leaked gemini agents aged 16-26h consuming ~31GB of swap, even though gemini is NOT used for real work and GEMINI_API_KEY is unset. Killing them dropped swap 35G-&gt;4.2G.&#xA;ROOT CAUSE: gemini is in PreferenceOrder (internal/harnesses/registry.go:6) and registered (registry.go:82-87, DefaultModel gemini-2.5-flash). The gemini harness discovery/quota path (internal/harnesses/gemini/model_discovery.go, quota_pty.go, runner.go) spawns gemini via PTY for model/quota discovery and does not terminate/reap the spawned process group on completion/timeout. progress_mirror.go:65 references a &#39;stale-harness reaper&#39; for orphaned process groups, but it is not catching these discovery/quota probes. ddx&#39;s own integration test documents the leak: cli/internal/integration/lock_contention_test.go:226-229 (in the ddx repo) notes &#39;ddx work harness discovery probes whatever agent CLIs are installed (codex/claude/gemini), and at least one (gemini) [lingers] ... racing t.TempDir cleanup&#39;.&#xA;PROPOSED FIX: (a) do NOT probe/discover a harness that is unconfigured/uncredentialed (skip gemini when GEMINI_API_KEY is unset / not enabled) so it is never spawned; and (b) ensure the discovery/quota PTY probe sets a process group (Setpgid) and kills it on completion AND timeout, wired into the stale-harness reaper.
